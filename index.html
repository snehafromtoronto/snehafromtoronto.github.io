About

I'm an engineering leader focused on developer platforms, AI-powered developer experiences, CI/CD systems, and enterprise software — with 15+ years of experience across Microsoft, GitHub, and Amazon.

Today I lead enterprise AI and developer-platform initiatives across Microsoft's Developer organization, accelerating GitHub Copilot adoption and establishing reusable engineering patterns for AI-assisted development. Before that, I spent three years at GitHub as a Senior Partner Engineer, working on Copilot Extensions, the Model Context Protocol (MCP), and AI-powered developer tooling.

I care about the whole system: the platforms engineers build on, the teams that build them, and the practices — measurement, enablement, governance, and feedback loops — that turn tools into real outcomes. I've led and mentored engineers across the US, India, Europe, and beyond, and I believe great platforms come from great teams.
