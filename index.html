// About

About

I'm fascinated by what happens when AI stops being a tool developers reach for and starts becoming a collaborator that works alongside them. Agentic developer experiences, where agents plan, act, and use real tools through protocols like MCP, are changing how software gets built, and I want to help shape that change so it actually works for developers.

Most of my energy goes into making these ideas concrete and approachable. I write about what makes AI-assisted development succeed or stall, from platform thinking to the practices that turn promising demos into everyday workflows. I also love getting on stage, whether that's a conference talk, a panel, or a hallway conversation that runs long.

Talking with developers is how I learn the most. If you're building agents, rethinking your developer platform, or putting together a lineup on the future of software development, I'd love to hear from you.
